Sourdough starter basics

A sourdough starter is a mix of flour and water colonised by wild yeast and lactic acid bacteria. Feed it equal weights of flour and water once a day at room temperature.

A healthy starter roughly doubles in volume four to eight hours after feeding and smells pleasantly sour. If a grey liquid called hooch forms on top, the starter is hungry: pour it off and feed it.
