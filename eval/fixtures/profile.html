Isla Brennan

Isla Brennan is a Scottish hill runner and coach with Ochil Hill Runners (fictional test page).

Born14 March 1990, Dollar, Clackmannanshire
ClubOchil Hill Runners (joined 2011)
Best resultBen Ledi race, first place, 2019

She began coaching junior runners in 2016 and has led the club's winter training programme since 2021.
